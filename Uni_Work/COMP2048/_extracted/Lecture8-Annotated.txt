Sensitivity of TIME(f (n)) to Model of Computation
Unfortunately TIME(f (n)) depends on the model of computation
Example: for L = {0k 1k : k > 0},

with one-tape TMs L 2 TIME(n2 )
with two-tape TMs L 2 TIME(n)

But this dependence is at most polynomial:
e.g., any t(n)-time multi-tape TM (when t(n) > n) can
be simulated by a one-tape TM in time O(t(n)2 )
We may only get polynomial slowdowns/speedups by switching
between reasonable deterministic models of computations
