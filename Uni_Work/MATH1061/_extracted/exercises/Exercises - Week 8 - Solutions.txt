(7) (2 marks) Is g ◦ g is bijective? Justify your answer.
The function g ◦ g is bijective. First, to simplify our working, notice that
(g ◦ g)(x) = (x + 2) + 2 = x + 4.
To show g ◦ g is injective, suppose that (g ◦ g)(x) = (g ◦ g)(y) for some integers
x and y. Then x + 4 = y + 4, so x = y and thus g ◦ g is injective.
To show g ◦ g is surjective, take any y ∈ Z. Then (g ◦ g)(y − 4) = (y − 4) + 4 = y,
and hence g ◦ g is surjective.
As g ◦ g is both injective and surjective, it is bijective.
