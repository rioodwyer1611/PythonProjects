(a) (2 marks) Use the Euclidean Algorithm to compute gcd(252, 198).
252 = 1 × 198 + 54
198 = 3 × 54 + 36
54 = 1 × 36 + 18
36 = 2 × 18 = 0
Therefore, we have gcd(252, 198) = 18.

(b) (2 marks) Find the prime factorisation of 252 and 198.
252 = 2 × 126 = 2 × 2 × 63 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 21 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 × 7 = 22 × 32 × 7
198 = 2 × 99 = 2 × 3 × 33 = 2 × 3 × 3 × 11 = 2 × 32 × 11
Therefore, the prime factorisation of 252 is 22 × 32 × 7 and the prime factorisation of 198 is 2 × 32 × 11.
