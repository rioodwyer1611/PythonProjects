unique integers q and r such that n = dq + r and 0 ≤ r < d.

prime numbers p1 , p2 , . . . , pk , and positive integers e1 , e2 , . . . , ek such that n = pe11 pe22 · · · pekk , and any
other expression for n as a product of prime numbers is identical to this except perhaps for the order
in which the factors are written.
